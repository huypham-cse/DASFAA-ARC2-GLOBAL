Where the evidence draws the boundary

All six independent Procrustes point differences are negative, with intervals crossing zero. All-paid FiQA/E5 loses to Procrustes and LA2M; MiniLM versus ridge is unresolved. Actual mixed replacement can reverse proxy-only ordering. E5 native Full-X can exceed Full-Y, so retaining an incumbent may be the better quality choice.

ARC2 is a candidate for a required destination-space migration with a small declared fit set and a preference for a compact non-neural map. The actual endpoint and relevance/Recall targets should determine adoption. Physical migration cost, SLO, ANN and real traffic are unmeasured.
