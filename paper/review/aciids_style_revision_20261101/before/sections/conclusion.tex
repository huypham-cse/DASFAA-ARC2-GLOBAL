\section{Conclusion}
\method\ improves a compact document adapter by fitting two tangent residual corrections to a Procrustes initialization. The updates preserve affine inference while targeting prediction direction for normalized retrieval. Broad development comparisons, mechanism ablations, matched all-paid fitting and a prospective collection identify useful retrieval gains and encoder-dependent transfer. Together with the mixed-gallery diagnostics, these results support directional correction as a practical adapter design and mixed-endpoint evaluation as a necessary part of staged embedding migration.
