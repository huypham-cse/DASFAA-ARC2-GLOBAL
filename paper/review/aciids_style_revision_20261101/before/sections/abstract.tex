\begin{abstract}
Embedding-model upgrades require legacy document vectors to remain searchable while a new representation is introduced. We present \method, a document adapter that fits two closed-form, norm-scaled negative cosine-gradient targets while retaining a centered Procrustes initialization. Both corrections collapse into one affine map, allowing native destination queries to search mapped and newly encoded documents together. Across four development collections, two encoder migrations and three destination-document budgets, \method\ improves macro \ndcg\ over all five evaluated baseline configurations. It exceeds Procrustes in 23 of 24 condition means, with a macro gain of 0.0243 and Bonferroni-adjusted interval [0.0181, 0.0308]. Ablations establish an additional 0.0069 development gain from the second correction. On prospective TREC-COVID/E5, gains over the evaluated Converter range from 0.2000 to 0.2572 across the three budgets. On MiniLM, 75\% coverage gives 0.5195 nDCG versus native Full-Y's 0.5203. The final 384-to-768-dimensional adapter occupies 1.13\,MiB, uses 114.7 times fewer parameter bytes than that Converter, and requires no neural mapper training.
\keywords{Embedding migration \and Vector retrieval \and Representation alignment \and Mixed galleries}
\end{abstract}
