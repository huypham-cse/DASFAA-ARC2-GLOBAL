    ARC2-Global: Tangent-Corrected Alignment
        for Embedding-Index Migration

                             Anonymous Author(s)

                             Anonymous Institution



      Abstract. Embedding-model upgrades must preserve retrieval quality
      while document re-encoding proceeds under a limited budget. We pro-
      pose ARC2-Global, a compact document adapter that refines centered
      Procrustes alignment with two ridge-fitted tangent corrections. The
      updates target cosine-relevant directions and collapse into one affine
      map, preserving native destination queries without neural mapper
      training. Across four development collections, two encoder migrations
      and three anchor-inclusive budgets, ARC2-Global achieves the highest
      macro nDCG@10 among six evaluated methods. Its gain over Procrustes
      is 0.0243, with family-adjusted interval [0.0181, 0.0308]; the second
      correction adds 0.0069. On prospective TREC-COVID/E5, gains over
      the evaluated Converter range from 0.2000 to 0.2572. A descriptive
      comparison across all five collections further shows E5 ARC2 at 25%
      coverage exceeding Converter and affine ridge at 75%. On TREC-
      COVID/MiniLM, 75% coverage yields 0.5195 nDCG versus native
      Full-Y’s 0.5203, with the highest Recall@100 point mean among the six
      adapters. The final 384-to-768-dimensional coefficient matrix occupies
      1.13 MiB, using 114.7 times fewer parameter bytes than the evaluated
      Converter.

      Keywords: Embedding migration · Vector retrieval · Representation
      alignment · Mixed galleries


1   Introduction
Embedding-model upgrades create a practical index-maintenance problem:
destination-model queries must remain effective while legacy document vectors
are replaced gradually. A staged migration learns from paired source and
destination embeddings, maps the remaining documents, and replaces these
proxies as true destination vectors become available. The useful operating point
is an intermediate index that delivers strong retrieval quality with limited
destination-document exposure.
    The intermediate index must rank true destination embeddings and mapped
proxies together. A mapper that works well on a proxy-only gallery can change
behavior after true vectors are inserted. Cosine retrieval also depends on direc-
tion, whereas coordinate regression spends capacity on radial errors that disap-
pear after normalization. This motivates a directional document adapter that
preserves compact inference and searches with the native destination query.
2         Anonymous Author(s)

    Document-side conversion and query-side adaptation already provide routes
across embedding spaces [17,11]. Orthogonal alignment supplies a compact geo-
metric starting point, while local alignment and neural regression provide more
flexible alternatives [16,2]. We investigate a complementary design: correcting
the prediction error that changes vector direction while retaining one affine doc-
ument map.
    ARC2-Global applies two tangent corrections to a centered rectangular Pro-
crustes map. For each paired document, it projects destination error onto the
tangent plane at the predicted direction, rescales it to the raw prediction, and
fits an affine ridge update. Repeating this operation twice yields coefficients
that collapse into one affine map followed by normalization. Native destination
queries then search true and proxy vectors together, with each newly encoded
document replacing its proxy under the same identifier (Fig. 1).
    The results identify a strong quality–exposure trade-off. On four development
collections, the fixed 1,024-pair adapter leads all five baseline configurations in
macro nDCG@10 across two migrations and three budgets, gaining 0.0243 over
Procrustes. The second correction adds 0.0069 without adding an inference stage.
On the prospective TREC-COVID collection, E5 gains over Converter and ridge
persist at every budget. Across all five collections, E5 ARC2 at 25% coverage
also exceeds these two alternatives at 75% in a descriptive comparison. Matched
all-paid fitting strengthens MiniLM adaptation by another 0.0596 nDCG at the
same paid-document budget.
Our contributions are:

    – Directional refinement with affine inference. Two tangent-residual
      ridge updates target prediction direction and improve measured development
      retrieval while retaining one compact document map and native destination
      queries.
    – Strong quality at matched fitting exposure. Across two migrations
      and three paid-document budgets, the four-collection development macro
      favors ARC2-Global over all five evaluated alternatives. Ablations quantify
      the value of the second update, and all-paid fitting tests the method beyond
      fixed-anchor supervision.
    – Useful quality at lower document exposure. A held-collection evalua-
      tion confirms E5 gains over Converter and affine ridge at each tested budget.
      The descriptive 25%-versus-75% comparison exposes a useful migration op-
      erating point, while mixed-gallery diagnostics explain why adapter choice
      must be evaluated after native-vector insertion.

Compatibility and translation direction. Backward-compatible representation
learning trains a new encoder to remain usable with previously computed gallery
features [9]. Drift-Adapter instead fits a post-hoc map from new-model queries
to an existing legacy index, comparing Procrustes, low-rank affine, and residual
neural adapters [11]. Spherical interpolation refines Procrustes-aligned queries
4       Anonymous Author(s)

through SLERP between old and aligned new query directions [7]; ARC2 instead
fits tangent ridge updates to document anchors. ARC2-Global shares the use of
paired embeddings and frozen encoders, but maps legacy document vectors into
the destination space. Its evaluation concerns mapped and native destination
documents competing as re-encoding progresses.

Geometric alignment and refinement. Orthogonal Procrustes provides the clas-
sical least-squares alignment underlying our initialization [8]. Recent work con-
nects approximate preservation of pairwise inner products to bounds on Pro-
crustes alignment error and demonstrates interoperability across retrieval mod-
els [5]. Unpaired translation pursues a different supervision regime: vec2vec
learns translation through a shared latent representation without known cor-
respondences [4], while mini-vec2vec combines pseudo-pair construction, Pro-
crustes estimation, and iterative matching and clustering refinements [1]. Thus,
linear translation, Procrustes initialization, and refinement are all established
ingredients. ARC2-Global uses known document correspondences and a specific
finite refinement: exactly two ridge-fitted tangent corrections to a centered rect-
angular Procrustes initialization. Both corrections are folded into one affine co-
efficient matrix, followed by row normalization at application time. This differs
from discovering pseudo-correspondences, learning a latent translator, or routing
documents among local maps.

Retrieval evaluation. BEIR emphasizes retrieval across heterogeneous tasks and
domains [10], while NanoBEIR provides smaller collections suitable for develop-
ment comparisons [18]. Sentence-BERT, MiniLM, and E5 supply established en-
coder families and training approaches relevant to our model transitions [6,14,13].
Our evaluation targets judged retrieval quality in actual mixed galleries, sep-
arating that quantity from agreement with a destination ranking or approxi-
mate nearest-neighbor overlap. This distinction matters because accurately re-
constructing individual vectors and preserving their internal neighborhoods do
not directly measure the relevance of the final combined ranking. The exper-
imental question is therefore whether the correction improves retrieval under
a matched encoding budget, and in which encoder and gallery conditions that
improvement persists.


3     ARC2-Global: Angular Refinement for Mixed Galleries

ARC2-Global converts legacy document vectors into destination-space proxies
and combines them with true destination vectors. Two direction-focused correc-
tions refine one global affine map.


3.1   Migration Setting and Budget
Let a corpus contain N documents with stored, unit-normalized source embed-
dings xi ∈ Rds , written as row vectors. The destination encoder produces unit

vectors yi ∈ Rdt , but obtaining every yi requires encoding the entire corpus again.
We seek destination-space document representations using only a budgeted set of
destination document vectors. The dimensions may differ; our evaluated encoder
pairs map ds = 384 to dt = 768. Queries are encoded directly by the destination
query encoder; the document map aligns with that existing destination query
space.
   Let A be the paired anchor set used for fitting and S the set selected for
document replacement. The unique destination-document budget is

                           b = |A ∪ S|,      b = ⌊ρN ⌋.                         (1)

Anchors consume this budget. Our selection starts with A and fills the remain-
ing slots from a fixed random document order, skipping duplicates, so A ⊆ S
and |S| = b. Larger budgets extend the same order and retain the same fitted
anchors. The principal setting uses m = |A| = 1,024 pairs; an all-paid-pairs
comparison separately fits on every paid pair. Fit uses paired document vec-
tors without queries or relevance labels. This separates the amount of fitting
supervision from the number of true vectors in the gallery. The same map serves
several replacement budgets; after full replacement, scores depend entirely on
the destination encoder.

This fit centers its correction penalty on the current affine map while matching

a common coefficient scale, not arbitrary per-document changes or guaranteed
finite-step cosine improvement.

3.5   Computation and Storage
The final mapper has pdt coefficients regardless of anchor count. For 384 → 768,
this is 295,680 float32 values, or 1,182,720 bytes (1.13 MiB), including the inter-
cept. This is coefficient storage; fitting objects, document galleries, and encoder
weights are additional. Application requires one affine multiplication and nor-
malization per document, with O(N pdt ) arithmetic for a complete proxy gallery.
    Fitting cost depends on the solver backend. Beyond the cross-covariance and
rectangular SVD, costing O(mds dt ) and O(ds dt min(ds , dt )), respectively, the
reference dual implementation forms an m×m Gram matrix in O(m2 p) time, fac-
tors it in O(m3 ), and spends O(m2 dt + mpdt ) per correction. Its Gram storage is
O(m2 ). A separately identified primal backend solves (X e ⊤Xe + λIp )∆t = X e ⊤ T (t)

instead, with O(mp + p ) construction and factorization and O(p ) Gram stor-
age; it supports larger paired sets. Each primal correction costs O(mpdt + p2 dt ).
The equations are equivalent in exact arithmetic, while floating-point rankings
can differ near ties. Updates use float64 and the final coefficient is stored as
float32. Finally, |A ∪ S| measures logical destination-vector exposure in cached-
vector evaluations. It does not measure physical encoding work, deployment
latency, or end-to-end migration savings.

4     Experimental Setup

We ask three questions: does angular refinement improve fixed-supervision align-
ment, how does it compare with other mappers at a matched encoding budget,
and which gains transfer to a prospectively held collection? All experiments use
exact retrieval, isolating representation quality from approximate-index effects.

Collections and migrations. Development uses FiQA (57,638 documents, 500
development queries), NanoNQ (5,035/50), NanoHotpotQA (5,090/50), and
NanoFEVER (4,996/50), drawn from BEIR and NanoBEIR [10,18]. FiQA
informed method selection after its scores were inspected; all four collections are
development evidence. We subsequently fixed a separate confirmation on the
BEIR release of TREC-COVID [12,10]. Its input manifest contains all 171,332
documents and 50 queries. For this study, labels remained closed until every
ranking and endpoint had been sealed and separately verified. This distinguishes
development evidence from the prospective test. We evaluate two 384-to-768-
dimensional migrations: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 to all-distilroberta-v1, and
e5-small-v2 to e5-base-v2.1 Model revisions and input hashes are pinned.
Text encoding uses normalized float32 vectors, a maximum of 128 tokens, and
the E5 query/passage prefixes.

Matched supervision and exposure. The main matrix crosses four development
collections, two migrations, three paid-document budgets (25/50/75%), six
methods, and seeds 17, 29, 41, 53, and 67. All methods fit the same ordered
1,024 paired anchors and replace the same paid document rows. Paid sets
contain all anchors and are nested, with exactly ⌊ρN ⌋ unique target document
vectors; the accounting is |A∪S|, including anchors. On the Nano collections and
TREC-COVID, independent seeded streams sample anchors without replace-
ment and a document permutation; unseen permutation rows fill the budget.
FiQA retains its historical anchor/order arrays. Neither fitting nor selection
uses relevance labels. Paid nonanchor vectors provide replacement only in this
fixed-anchor regime. A separate FiQA experiment holds the 50% paid set fixed
and compares fitting on 1,024 versus all 28,819 paid pairs. All-paid ARC2 fits
use the float64 primal backend in Sect. 3.5; their fixed-anchor comparisons reuse
the dual-backend rankings. Both implement the same two-update rule.

Comparison methods. We retain the full five-baseline panel: centered rectangu-
lar Procrustes (an A2M adaptation); LA2M with 20 source-anchor clusters, local
alignment for clusters with at least two anchors and global fallback for singletons;
SimpleLinear; affine ridge with λ = 10 and a regularized bias; and Embedding-
Converter [16,2,17]. SimpleLinear uses pinned official code: despite its name, the
locked configuration is a five-layer SELU MLP, trained for the final 50 epochs
with batch size 4,028 and the MSE plus cosine loss, without qrel-based stopping.
Converter is a paper-specification adaptation with a 50,000-update selection run,

Retrieval and inference. Destination queries search a single gallery containing

query/document encoders throughout and provide reference operating points.
Our primary metric is graded nDCG@10 with gain 2rel − 1; auxiliary Recall@100
measures coverage over all positive judgments, including positives outside a Nano
corpus. All queries count; unjudged documents are nonrelevant. The prospec-

manuscript-source package.
    Each locked analysis uses 20,000 paired query-bootstrap draws after aver-
aging the five mapping seeds. Development macro effects weight the 24 collec-
tion/migration/budget conditions equally and resample queries within each col-
lection. Within each analysis, the same sampled query indices are shared across
methods, budgets and migrations for that collection. Saved 99% percentile inter-
vals give approximate 95% coverage for the five-comparison family; the ablations
have a separate five-contrast family. The all-paid experiment uses its predeclared
32-contrast family. TREC-COVID has 30 primary contrasts (five baselines, two
migrations, three budgets), with approximately 99.8333% individual percentile
intervals for approximate 95% family coverage. Its independent units are the
50 queries. Recall intervals are nominal 95% descriptive intervals. An interval
touching zero is unresolved. These intervals condition on the five observed map-
ping seeds and fixed collections and encoder pairs; development intervals do not
adjust for method selection.2

5.2    The Second Correction Adds Value without an Inference Stage
A second correction adds 0.0069 macro nDCG beyond one step, with family
interval [0.0038, 0.0100]. The point gain appears in 22 of 24 conditions and in
each of the five seed-level macros. Recomputing the tangent target therefore
provides a repeatable development benefit while the final mapper remains one
affine stage. The complete initialized refinement gains 0.0957 over affine ridge
(Fig. 3); this comparison jointly evaluates tangent targeting and the retained
alignment prior.
    The penalty controls favor moderate regularization. Changing λ from 10 to 1
gives +0.0032, with an interval crossing zero; changing it to 100 gives −0.0093,
with a wholly negative interval. The evaluated method keeps λ = 10 and exactly
two corrections.

6    Practical Value of Directional Adaptation
Compact inference with stronger development ranking. The 295,680 float32 coef-
ficients occupy 1.13 MiB, or 114.7 times fewer parameter bytes than the evaluated
Converter. Both corrections are absorbed into this matrix. Affine ridge has the
same coefficient count; refinement provides the measured quality benefit, while
affine mapping provides compactness.

When the adapter is useful. ARC2 targets required destination-model queries
during staged document replacement. E5 demonstrates useful quality at limited
               Tangent-Corrected Alignment for Embedding-Index Migration             15

exposure; MiniLM benefits from fitting all paid pairs without increasing docu-
ment coverage. Procrustes and LA2M remain competitive on the held collection,
so deployment choice should follow encoder-specific mixed-gallery validation.
When source queries remain acceptable, Full-X is also viable: all 24 partial-
development five-seed ARC2 condition means are below it.

Efficiency in the measured units. Destination-vector exposure, coefficient storage
and the absence of neural mapper training describe the demonstrated resource
advantages. Physical encoding time, index resources and serving SLOs require
deployment measurements.


7   Conclusion
ARC2-Global turns two tangent ridge corrections into one compact affine
adapter. It leads five alternatives in development macro nDCG, transfers sub-
stantial E5 gains over Converter and ridge to TREC-COVID, and exposes
useful 25%-versus-75% quality–coverage operating points. Stronger all-paid
MiniLM adaptation and measured replacement effects further show why migra-
tion should be evaluated in the mixed gallery that serves destination queries.
Angular refinement offers a practical route to budgeted index migration.
