## Assessment

ARC2-Global is a coherent, implementable small-method paper with a defensible specific contribution: two ridge-fitted, norm-scaled tangent targets added to centered rectangular Procrustes, retaining one affine map at inference. The derivation and implementation agree. The manuscript is unusually clear about inspected development evidence, matched-supervision adaptations, native references, unresolved prospective comparisons, and logical exposure versus physical cost. It has a real research result and should proceed as a compact mapper study.

The material rejection risk is contribution strength and utility, rather than an identified mathematical error. The strongest improvement over the closest same-size baseline is development-only; all six prospective differences from Procrustes have negative point estimates. Beating the declared Converter/ridge configurations prospectively is useful but does not independently establish the benefit of tangent correction over a simpler geometric adapter. The exact finite update is distinguishable from the literature, while the broader conversion, staged replacement, mixed-gallery, compact linear-map, and angular-refinement themes are already occupied. Those facts should remain visible in the main story.

## Correct strengths

1. The normalization differential is correct for nonzero predictions. The target is perpendicular to the current direction, its norm factor has the stated first-order meaning, and the manuscript appropriately avoids a convergence guarantee. Penalizing the update including its intercept, rather than the entire accumulated map, matches code.
2. Rectangular SVD orientation, raw affine expansion, two updates, lambda 10, float64 updates and float32 final coefficients are consistent with source. The 295,680 coefficients and 1,182,720-byte storage calculation are correct. Collapsing the coefficients gives a genuinely simple inference artifact, although Procrustes and ridge are compact too.
3. The 24-condition development macro result, second-step control, all-paid comparison, prospective 30-contrast family, and replacement diagnostic answer distinct questions. Native Full-X and Full-Y are retained, and the unsuccessful independent Procrustes comparisons are stated plainly. Nothing in these results warrants deleting negative evidence.
4. The paper evaluates relevance in the actual mixed gallery, rather than presenting destination-ranking agreement as relevance quality. The matched paid sets count anchors. Queries and labels do not enter fitting. These are appropriate controls for the claimed representation comparison.

## Literature assessment

The exact update is not found in the inspected nearest work. The paper should explicitly retain that narrow algorithmic delta. Its direct scientific neighbors are [LA2M](https://doi.org/10.1145/3769803), [HMoE](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/yang26e.html), [Embedding-Converter](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1237/), and [Drift-Adapter](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.805/). The first two already address integration/mixing; Converter already performs paired document conversion; Drift-Adapter establishes a practical alternative direction with affine and residual adapters. None should be dismissed merely by the words “document side” or “mixed gallery.”

[FastFill](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.04766), especially section 4.2, is the main omission because it actually replaces transformed gallery features with true new features. Its supervised neural/uncertainty objective differs from ARC2's paired-only finite affine correction. [mini-vec2vec](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.02348) already combines Procrustes and iterative refinement under unknown correspondences; its refinement rematches objects rather than predicting tangent targets on fixed pairs. [Spherical Interpolation for Backward-Compatible Multimodal Representations](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39836) explicitly addresses residual angular error after Procrustes, using query-side interpolation between two representations rather than a fitted document-side update. This recent preprint is relevant context, not an exact duplicate or a compulsory new baseline.
