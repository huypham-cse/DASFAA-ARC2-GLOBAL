\begin{abstract}
Embedding-model upgrades must preserve retrieval quality while document re-encoding proceeds under a limited budget. We propose \method, a compact document adapter that refines centered Procrustes alignment with two ridge-fitted tangent corrections. The updates target cosine-relevant directions and collapse into one affine map, preserving native destination queries without neural mapper training. Across four development collections, two encoder migrations and three anchor-inclusive budgets, \method\ achieves the highest macro \ndcg\ among six evaluated methods. Its gain over Procrustes is 0.0243, with family-adjusted interval [0.0181, 0.0308]; the second correction adds 0.0069. On prospective TREC-COVID/E5, gains over the evaluated Converter range from 0.2000 to 0.2572. A descriptive comparison across all five collections further shows E5 ARC2 at 25\% coverage exceeding Converter and affine ridge at 75\%. On TREC-COVID/MiniLM, 75\% coverage yields 0.5195 nDCG versus native Full-Y's 0.5203, with the highest Recall@100 point mean among the six adapters. The final 384-to-768-dimensional coefficient matrix occupies 1.13\,MiB, using 114.7 times fewer parameter bytes than the evaluated Converter.
\keywords{Embedding migration \and Vector retrieval \and Representation alignment \and Mixed galleries}
\end{abstract}
