## Method being checked

The frozen `src/arc2_global/model.py` computes a centered rectangular Procrustes map from ordered paired document anchors, expands it into an affine coefficient matrix, and adds exactly two tangent-target ridge updates with lambda=10. It applies a single resulting affine map and row normalization to legacy vectors; already encoded destination rows replace selected rows, including all anchors. Fit accepts neither queries nor relevance judgments. The claim here is the finite computation and its supported retrieval findings, not invention of Procrustes, ridge regression, post-hoc conversion, or mixed databases.

## Provisional synthesis

**PROCEED WITH CAUTION (local synthesis, not independent review).** No inspected paper was found to contain the exact two-step tangent ridge computation and its specific frozen mixed-gallery evidence. However, every broad ingredient has close precedent, and the exact technical delta is thin enough that a paper must explain the normalized directional residual target and show what the finite refinement reveals. Neither a claim of first document migration, first mixed-gallery evaluation, nor discovery that partial replacement can hurt is defensible. FastFill and the September2026 SLERP paper, surfaced by the parent's fresh review and then verified here, make those limitations explicit. There is no named exact-duplicate paper supporting an ABANDON verdict.

Suggested one-sentence positioning: *We evaluate a fixed, compact affine document-migration adapter that refines centered rectangular Procrustes with two tangent ridge corrections, and characterize its quality and rank-competition behavior when mapped legacy documents and native destination documents share an exact-search gallery under a matched paid-document budget.*

Key risks for the parent review: (1) the two-step rule can look like an ordinary finite optimization heuristic; show its computation and ablation evidence without claiming unique optimality; (2) independent confirmation does not establish a reliable advantage over Procrustes/LA2M; retain their unresolved comparisons; (3) adapting Converter/LA2M/SimpleLinear changes training regimes, so distinguish original papers from budget adaptations; (4) no HMoE or FastFill comparison means no broad best-translator/SOTA claim; (5) an affine coefficient count is structural storage evidence, not end-to-end systems cost; (6) converted/native score competition must be measured from actual rankings, not inferred from vector cosine alone; (7) FastFill already documents initial replacement harm, while SLERP studies post-Procrustes angular refinement and partial backfilling, so neither is a general discovery claim here.
