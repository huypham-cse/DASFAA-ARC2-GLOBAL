### GCPA equation check

GCPA's Appendix D has consensus `c_i = N(mean_m N(x_mi Omega_m))`, corrector `T_theta(u) = N(N(u) + Delta_theta(N(u)))`, and objective `E[1 - <T_theta(u), c_i>] + lambda E[max(0, 1 - <T_theta(u), N(u)> - tau)]`. Its inference composes universe rotation, the learned nonlinear corrector, and optionally a target rotation. These equations were verified in both arXiv v2 and the official proceedings PDF.

ARC2 instead has `T_j = ||z_j|| [y_j - <N(z_j), y_j> N(z_j)]`, then solves `min_D ||Xtilde D - T||² + 10 ||D||²` twice, with `B <- B + D`. Its prior is the current **coefficient matrix**, not a threshold on output angular drift; its target is the **destination anchor**, not a multi-view consensus. Directional correction and retaining a Procrustes frame therefore overlap conceptually, but neither the target, regularizer, parameterization nor inference is identical. This difference follows from the equations, not from the papers using different names. GCPA should be cited prominently.
