## Summary and recommendation

ARC2-Global initializes a document adapter with centered rectangular Procrustes and applies exactly two ridge-fitted, norm-scaled tangent corrections. These corrections accumulate into one affine map followed by normalization. Native destination queries search a gallery containing both proxies and paid destination vectors, with anchors counted in the encoding budget.

The contribution is credible and sufficiently useful for a weak accept under its current scope. The four-collection development macro improvement over Procrustes is substantial enough to matter (+0.0243 nDCG), and the second correction contributes +0.0069. Prospective TREC-COVID confirms large E5 advantages over the evaluated Converter and affine-ridge configurations. Those are valid positive results even though ARC2 does not dominate all methods. The score stops below accept because the incremental methodological novelty and absence of held nDCG gains over Procrustes leave the general practical advantage of the new refinement uncertain. Presentation polish does not resolve that scientific limitation.

## Ranked strengths

1. **A precise, compact computation with a defensible directional motivation.** Equations (4)-(8), PDF pp. 6-7, make the method concrete. The derivative is correct for nonzero predictions; the target is a norm-scaled negative cosine gradient, and the paper correctly states that fitted row updates need not remain tangent or improve cosine loss at every finite step. Retaining one coefficient matrix is a real inference simplification, although affine compactness itself is shared with ridge.
2. **Useful measured retrieval improvements with honest scope.** Development includes two migrations, four collections, three budgets and five seeds with matched anchors/replacement sets. The 0/1/2-step controls support the second correction's development value. Held E5 gains over Converter and ridge occur at every budget, including +0.2572 over Converter at 25%. They should be credited without requiring universal dominance.
3. **Good experimental discipline and visible negative evidence.** Exact search isolates representation effects; anchor-inclusive accounting prevents free-supervision budgeting. The paper distinguishes selected development evidence from the prospective collection, conditions intervals on the observed seeds, labels cross-budget comparisons as descriptive, shows all five baselines/native references and discloses the all-paid E5 deterioration. Saved per-query scores independently reproduce every saved condition mean in the three main summary families inspected, with maximum absolute error zero.
4. **The gallery diagnostics provide a meaningful operational lesson.** At fixed all-paid E5 fitting, replacement lowers nDCG from 0.3149 to 0.2919 and Recall from 0.6542 to 0.5595. This is useful evidence that proxy-only quality does not determine mixed-gallery quality. The paper appropriately treats partial backfilling and possible deterioration as established context rather than a newly discovered setting.

## Ranked weaknesses

1. **The refinement's independent advantage over the strongest cheap baseline is not demonstrated.** On prospective TREC-COVID, Procrustes has higher nDCG point means in all six cells; every Procrustes/LA2M family interval crosses zero. The paper discloses this on p. 12. Consequently, the held results establish usefulness versus the evaluated neural/ridge configurations, not a confirmed reason to prefer ARC2 over geometric alignment. This is a contribution-strength limitation, not a contradiction of the actual claims.
2. **Novelty is narrow, and the controls do not isolate a general tangent-specific causal advantage.** Procrustes, iterative alignment refinement, directional objectives and partial backfilling have prior art. The exact two-update global affine procedure is distinguishable, but its ingredients are familiar. The ARC2-versus-ridge result combines initialization/prior retention and tangent targeting, as p. 11 explicitly admits; it cannot attribute the entire +0.0957 to tangent projection alone. No such stronger attribution is needed for the present claim.
3. **Scientific breadth and deployment significance remain bounded.** There is one prospectively held collection with 50 queries and two 384-to-768 encoder transitions. The development macro combines three tiny Nano collections with FiQA, and its intervals do not account for method selection or population variation over encoder pairs/collections. All 24 partial-development ARC2 means remain below native Full-X, and some destination encoders themselves underperform Full-X. This makes the required-destination-query assumption materially important. Document exposure and coefficient storage support resource statements; they do not establish physical migration savings or serving behavior.
4. **Baseline adaptation and artifact access limit the interpretation.** Converter's MAE-based stopping and paired-document-only training differ from its original retrieval-driven selection and broad training regime. That is a reasonable matched-supervision design, clearly disclosed on pp. 8-9, but the very large gains cannot be generalized to the original method's best attainable performance. HMoE is cited but not evaluated; the paper therefore does not establish a state-of-the-art comparison. The promised anonymous artifact link must also become usable for ordinary reviewers.

## Strongest rejection argument

This is a small refinement of a strong, classical baseline in an already studied migration setting. Its main advantage over Procrustes comes from development conditions involved in method construction. On the only prospective collection, the simpler geometric baseline has higher nDCG in every tested condition, while the large confirmed gains are against adapted neural and ridge baselines that are much weaker in this setting. The current experiments therefore demonstrate a sensible compact adapter, but provide limited independent evidence that its extra directional computation advances the best available migration operating point. Without a clearer magnitude or domain of advantage over existing geometric alignment, the contribution may be judged too incremental for a full research paper.

That is the strongest credible rejection case. I nevertheless lean weak accept because the finite method is transparent, its development gains are consistent, its held gains against declared alternatives are real, and the paper does not claim universal superiority or measured end-to-end savings.

## Minimum response / fixes and experiment decision

**No new experiment is necessary to substantiate the paper's actual claims.** The central assertions are descriptive development superiority, a measured second-step development gain, held E5 improvements against specified configurations, observed cross-budget operating points and exact coefficient storage. The inspected evidence supports those assertions. No fatal current-claim technical inconsistency or clear numerical overclaim was found in this review.

The minimum submission fix is to replace the pending artifact notice (p. 9, footnote 2) with an accessible anonymous artifact containing the promised baseline configuration, revisions, locks and analysis entry points. A short response should explicitly acknowledge that prospective nDCG improvement over Procrustes is unresolved; that admission is already in the body, so this needs emphasis rather than another experiment. Also preserve the distinction between the full initialized refinement and an isolated benefit of tangent targeting.

More independent collections/encoder transitions, a direct tangent-versus-other-residual control with the same prior, an HMoE comparison, fit-supervision curves with matched effective regularization, or physical serving/migration measurements would strengthen a broader paper. They are **optional scientific extensions**, not required repairs to the present limited claims, and none was run or authorized by this review.
