Reuse existing document embeddings while moving an index into a new model space.

ARC2-Global combines a compact affine document adapter with two closed-form tangent corrections for staged embedding-index migration. With 1,024 paired fitting documents, it improves aggregate nDCG@10 over five declared baseline configurations on four development collections and achieves prospective TREC-COVID gains over the declared Converter, affine-ridge and SimpleLinear configurations.

A small adapter with measured retrieval gains

ARC2 learns from paired old/new document embeddings, applies two closed-form tangent corrections (λ=10), and compiles the result into an affine map plus normalization. Native destination vectors replace proxies as backfill proceeds. Queries stay in the destination space; each document has one index vector.

The final map stores 295,680 coefficients—114.7× fewer parameter bytes than the declared Converter MLP—and requires no neural training. This comparison concerns final mapping weights.

Prospective transfer across migration budgets

On TREC-COVID/E5, ARC2 has positive family intervals over Converter and affine ridge at 25%, 50% and 75% document budgets. Converter gains are +0.2572, +0.2314 and+0.2000 nDCG; the full curves below retain every declared method and native reference.

The gain is supported by mechanism and supervision controls

The second tangent correction adds+0.0069 macro nDCG beyond one step in the DEV ablation. In a separate matched all-paid FiQA/MiniLM study, ARC2 wins four of five family-adjusted comparisons when every method receives the same 28,819 fitting pairs; ridge is unresolved.

These findings connect the method design to retrieval gains and show a positive operating point beyond the main1,024-pair setting.

When ARC2 is useful

A new encoder must be introduced gradually, a compact non-neural forward adapter is valuable, and the application can evaluate relevance at its intended migration budget. ARC2 offers a simple final mapping with measured quality gains in the documented settings.

Gains vary with encoder pair and budget. The full evidence guide contains the complete outcome matrices and operational measurement scope.
