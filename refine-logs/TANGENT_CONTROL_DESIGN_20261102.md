# Matched-initialization tangent/residual control design

Date: 2026-11-02. Status: pre-result scientific design; pending executor feasibility/implementation review and immutable protocol commit. Internal same-family reviewer, not independent external validation. This document authorizes no run. No qrels or new results were examined; manuscript untouched.

The current scoring review correctly identifies a confound: ARC2 versus the existing zero-centered affine ridge changes both initialization/prior retention and the target. This finite development ablation can separate these choices. It cannot repair the lack of a held geometric-baseline advantage or establish general tangent superiority.

## Exact controls and recommendation

Use three scored methods: frozen **Tangent2**, named **NormScaledChordal2**, and named **EuclideanResidual2**. All start from the *identical* centered rectangular Procrustes raw affine coefficient B0. Let X=[source anchors,1], z=XB, r=||z||, u=z/r, y=unit destination anchor, c=<u,y>. At each of exactly two updates use:

| Method | Row target R | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Tangent2, unchanged | r(y-cu) | Frozen ARC2 reference |
| NormScaledChordal2 | r(y-u) | Does removing the radial component of this norm-scaled directional residual help? |
| EuclideanResidual2 | y-z = y-ru | Does tangent refinement outperform ordinary coordinate-residual fitting with the same retained map/prior? |

For every method, Delta=X.T solve(XX.T+10 I,R), B_next=B+Delta. Equivalently, minimize ||XC-(XB+R)||_F^2+10||C-B||_F^2. Penalize the intercept identically. Recompute R from each method's own B after update one. Do not reset to B0 at step two; do not normalize B or anchor predictions between updates. Keep float64 coefficients/solve/update arithmetic through both updates, export float32 B2 once, and use the existing float32 augmented-input affine application followed by row normalization. Reuse the dual factorization; no solver switch in this comparison. Use separate control names without changing the frozen API/revision.

**Chordal is an adequate isolation control, with precise limits.** P_u=I-u.T u gives P_u R_chordal=R_tangent and R_chordal-R_tangent=r(c-1)u. Thus, at a common state, only radial target content differs. Both retain positive global coefficient-scale equivariance. The term "norm-matched" must mean matching the prediction-norm factor r; the two target lengths are not matched: their squared lengths are 2r^2(1-c) and r^2(1-c^2). Do not rescale one target to equal the other's length: that introduces another choice. After the first update, trajectories differ; this compares two fixed algorithms, not a controlled single-step derivative effect. Shared ridge fitting can turn radial target differences into angular changes on other rows; fitted corrections need not be tangent.

**Euclidean residual is essential for the requested ordinary-residual question.** It is the natural coordinate regression control. P_u R_euclidean=R_tangent/r, so this comparison changes prediction-norm weighting as well as radial content; Euclidean is not globally scale equivariant around fixed unit y. A tangent win over it alone cannot isolate projection. A chordal win alone does not establish an advantage over ordinary Euclidean refinement. The joint three-row matrix answers both questions. If the intended three rows were B0/Tangent2/Chordal2, replace the scored B0 row with EuclideanResidual2; B0 is already computed and may be reused as an explicitly descriptive reference. If B0 must remain a primary scored row, Euclidean requires a prospectively declared fourth row, before results. Do not silently call chordal ordinary Euclidean residual.

## Hypotheses, paired inference and interpretation

Primary metric: nDCG@10, using the existing locked gain/discount/query-denominator rules. Four prespecified two-sided paired contrasts: Tangent2 minus Chordal2 and Tangent2 minus Euclidean2, separately for each encoder pair. Hypothesis under the tangent motivation: positive differences; negative and null outcomes are equally admissible. Do not require a minimum positive outcome to call execution complete.

For each query and method first average the five mapping seeds, then form paired differences. Resample query indices jointly across all methods and both pairs (20,000 draws, PCG64/default_rng seed 20261102; save indices or their hash, linear percentiles). Report point differences, nominal 95% descriptive CIs, and **98.75% Bonferroni percentile CIs** at quantiles 0.00625/0.99375 for this four-contrast nDCG family (approximate 95% family coverage). These four paired bootstrap contrast tests are primary; a family interval wholly above/below zero supports a conditional positive/negative difference; otherwise unresolved. Do not add unregistered p-value tests, pick nominal intervals, or select an encoder macro after results. Report every seed's paired mean, SD/range and query win/tie/loss counts; seeds are not 5Q independent queries. The equal-pair macro and Recall@100 can have nominal descriptive intervals only; no additional confirmatory family. Metric ties are exact equality of saved per-query values, with no tolerance used to manufacture wins.

These intervals condition on the five fixed seeds, sampled paired anchors/replacements, two encoder transitions and already-inspected development collection. They quantify query resampling uncertainty, not uncertainty over method selection, seeds, datasets or encoder populations. Crossing zero is neither equivalence nor noninferiority; no justified practical margin is set. A result favorable to tangent supports only these matched FiQA configurations. Even concordant wins do not establish a universal projection mechanism or an independent paper win. If tangent beats Euclidean but not chordal, attribute no isolated projection benefit. If it beats chordal but not Euclidean, report the norm-scaled comparison and absence of ordinary-residual superiority. Preserve all outcomes and stop before manuscript/method changes.

## Smoke, degeneracy and no-op checks before scoring

1. On deterministic synthetic unit pairs, independently verify B0 orientation/intercept/raw parity, the three target formulas, radial identity and u dot T=0; float64 algebra residuals should meet declared atol=rtol=1e-10 on well-conditioned fixtures. Existing tangent path must match the frozen implementation (per-step float64 allclose 1e-9; exported coefficient/proxy/score maxabs <=1e-6). Check actual ranking parity and report near-tie drift; do not loosen tolerances after results.
2. Verify each solve against its normal equation (relative residual <=1e-10 on well-conditioned synthetic fixtures), including intercept regularization; check independent primal/dual formula agreement as a test only. For Euclidean, the second update satisfies Delta1=10 (X.T X+10I)^(-1) Delta0; this catches accidentally reset or zero-centered fits. Verify two updates actually occur and recompute each method's targets.
3. Include aligned u=y with r unequal to one (T and C zero, E radial), an oblique nonzero-angle case (all expected differences visible), and antipodal y=-u (T zero while C/E need not be). Include shared nonorthogonal source rows and at least one unpaired held-out source row, so synthetic tests can detect an angular effect from the fitted radial difference rather than only anchor interpolation. Include a deliberately least-squares-stationary B0 fixture where X.T(Y-XB0)=0: Euclidean must correctly remain stationary. Do not assert every control must improve or differ on every fixture.
4. Check positive common B0 scaling leaves T/C normalized outputs invariant within declared numeric tolerance; expect no such identity for E. Check one/two-step snapshots, float64 retention, normalized inference, exact paid overwrite, anchors included in paid rows, selection counts and deterministic ties. Invalid zero/nonfinite directions use the existing <=1e-12 guard and record a failure, never an epsilon-denominator repair.
5. In qrel-free real-data smoke, record per-step r/c distributions, target radial/tangent energies, ||X.T R||, coefficient/update norms, relative normal-equation residual, proxy angular change on **unpaid** rows, changed scores and ordered rankings versus B0 and other arms. A nonzero R can be annihilated by X.T; nonzero coefficients can change only radial scale and vanish after normalization; paid overwrite can hide changed anchors. Audit all three levels. Check C-T is radial at a common diagnostic state, not falsely between diverged trajectories. Record alignment/antipodal frequencies rather than assuming they cannot occur.
6. An unexpected exact/no-near-effect identity across all nontrivial oblique/coupled fixtures is an implementation STOP. A legitimate no-op caused by stationarity, normalization, tiny angular error or paid overwrite is a scientific outcome, not permission to alter the control. If actual real-data smoke is degenerate, document its algebraic reason and STOP for design interpretation before quality scoring; no stronger claim is identifiable from a no-op contrast. Near-zero numerical diagnostics must be reported continuously; do not impose a post-hoc useful-effect threshold.

Smoke covers each method and both encoder-pair input paths without quality selection. Commit the full execution protocol/lock before smoke; record timing, RSS, versions, input/output hashes, failures and shared load. There is no arbitrary GPU/RAM/time cutoff and no defensible compute-hour estimate without this matched smoke. Fits require only cached vectors and linear algebra, no neural/GPU training. Executor should report empirical phase timings as planning observations; shared B0/factor timing is not a standalone method-cost estimate. Full completion must precede the separate scorer; then verify all tables from raw per-query outputs and STOP.
